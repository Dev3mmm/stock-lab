# ST02: Daily Turtle breakout 20/10, long only (stocks version of crypto S07)

**Source:** the trend-following idea that tested best in the crypto lab (S07). Included so stocks and crypto are compared on the same strategy.
**Status:** rules frozen 2026-09-27, before any run.

## Frozen rules
- Buy at the next open when the close is above the highest high of the previous 20 days. Exit at the next open when the close is below the lowest low of the previous 10 days. Initial stop 2 x ATR(20) below entry (exit at the stop, or at the open if it gaps). Long only, one position per asset.
- Costs, universe, split, baselines and pass bar: identical to ST01.
- Variant (pre-registered): ST02-B 55/20.
